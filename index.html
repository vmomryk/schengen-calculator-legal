1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes what information the mobile application Schengen Calculator ("App"), developed by VMSoft, collects, processes, and stores, and how that information is used. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

We take your privacy seriously and comply with the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable laws.

2. Data Collected and Processed

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device (localStorage) and does not transmit it to any servers:

  • Dates of previous stays in the Schengen Area (entered by you).
  • Planned entry date (entered by you).
  • Interface language preference (UA or EN).
  • Theme preference (light or dark).
We have no access to this data. All calculations are performed entirely on your device without any communication with external servers.

2.2 Data Collected by Google AdMob

The App may use Google AdMob advertising service to display banner advertisements. In this case, Google may collect and process the following data:

  • Device advertising identifier (Advertising ID).
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Device information (model, OS version).
  • Interaction data with advertisements.

This data is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. We do not have access to this data and do not control its collection.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Locally stored data is used exclusively for:

  • Performing the 90/180-day rule calculation.
  • Saving your entered dates between App sessions.
  • Restoring your preferences (language, theme) on the next launch.

4. Sharing Data with Third Parties

VMSoft does not sell, transfer, or disclose any personal user data to third parties, except for Google AdMob to the extent described in Section 2.2 of this Policy.

We do not use any other analytics services, tracking systems, or telemetry collection.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All local data is stored on your device indefinitely until deleted. You can delete all stored data at any time using the "Reset All" function in the App, or by uninstalling the App from your device.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

AccessObtain information about the data stored.
RectifyCorrect inaccurate data directly within the App.
EraseDelete all data via "Reset All" or by uninstalling the App.
ObjectOpt out of personalised advertising through your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us: info.vmsoft@gmail.com

7. Data Security

Since all user data is stored locally on your device, its security is provided by the protective mechanisms of the Android/iOS operating system. VMSoft is not responsible for unauthorised access to data resulting from device compromise.

8. Children

The App is not intended for persons under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us for deletion.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

VMSoft reserves the right to update this Policy. The current version is always available in the App menu and on Google Play. Continued use of the App after changes are made constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
